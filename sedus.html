02

Marketing Tool

Marketing / Event

Opening event — new showroom.

Together with the marketing team I designed postcards featuring the original sketches of the designer behind the se:living collection, celebrating the artistry and thoughtfulness that went into its creation. These sketches capture the raw, creative process — the moment when vision transforms into design. The postcards are intentionally made to resemble an unfinished colouring card, inviting people to imagine the pieces in any colour that suits their mood or space. They can even colour them in themselves, adding a personal touch and turning them into unique keepsakes to hang on the fridge or display as art. It's a tribute to the designer's creativity and a way to engage with the collection in a fun and interactive way.
