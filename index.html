About

An interior architect shaping the spaces where people spend their days.

I design workspaces and interiors from first floor plan to final finish — space planning, technical drawing, and the selection of materials, light and furniture. I trained in Interior Architecture at KU Leuven, with a semester in Cyprus and further study in Bali.

Alongside corporate interiors I work across showroom planning, set and event design, and floral installation — driven, structured and endlessly curious about how a space is put together.
